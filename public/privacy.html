Privacy Policy

Lumen Arc · Last updated:

Lumen Arc ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal life-management application that helps you track tasks, habits, calories, finances, and related personal data, with optional AI-assisted summaries. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Access

When you choose to connect your Google account, and only with your explicit consent, the app requests read-only access to your Gmail messages (gmail.readonly) and your Google account email address (userinfo.email). We use this access solely to generate a summary of your recent emails inside the app, at your request.

You may also enter personal data directly into the app (such as tasks, habits, calorie entries, and financial records). This data is stored in your own account.

How We Use Your Information

  • To display and organize the information you add to the app.
  • To generate email summaries when you request them, by processing the content of your recent emails.
  • To provide optional AI-generated summaries and insights.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than providing the app's features to you.

Google User Data

Lumen Arc's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Email content accessed via the Gmail API is used only to produce summaries you request and is not stored beyond what is necessary to display those summaries to you.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard cloud infrastructure. Access tokens are stored securely and used only to fetch data on your behalf. We take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your Choices

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from within the app, which removes our stored access to your Gmail. You may also stop using the app and request deletion of your data.
